About Us

The ISBAT Student ICT Training Centre provides training to students who want to improve their computer knowledge. We offer different programmes for beginners and students who want to improve their skills.

Students learn through practical lessons, demonstrations and group activities. Our aim is to help learners become confident when using technology.

Available Training Programmes

  • Web Development
  • Computer Networking
  • Database Management
  • Cybersecurity

Steps to Register
